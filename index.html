History of Women in Snowsports

Women have played an important role in the snowsports community for generations, from competitive athletes and professional instructors to coaches, technicians, photographers and industry leaders. Women in Snowsports celebrates the passion, determination and achievements of women across skiing and snowboarding. The sport continues to grow, creating more opportunities for women to participate, compete and build careers within the industry.

Community of Women in Snowsports

Women in Snowsports is about more than competition—it is about community, confidence and inspiring the next generation. By sharing the stories of female athletes, highlighting their achievements and showcasing opportunities within the industry, we hope to encourage more women and girls to get involved in snowsports. Whether you're a beginner discovering your love for the mountains or an experienced athlete chasing your next goal, there is a place for you on the snow.

Snowsports is a community built on passion, adventure and a love for the mountains. Women continue to make their mark across every part of the industry, breaking barriers and creating opportunities for those who follow. From Olympic and professional athletes to instructors, patrollers, coaches, technicians and industry professionals, women are helping shape the future of snowsports both on and off the mountain.

Women in Snowsports aims to shine a light on these women and the stories behind their journeys. Every athlete has a different path, with different challenges, goals and experiences, but their determination and love for the sport connects them all. By celebrating their achievements and sharing their experiences, we hope to inspire more women to step onto the snow, find their community and discover where their own journey can take them.
